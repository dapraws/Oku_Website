## Keeping a copy

Your journal lives on your iPhone. If iCloud sync is off and the iPhone is lost, replaced
or reset without a backup, your entries are lost with it. Turn on iCloud sync in Oku or
back up your iPhone to keep a copy. A deleted entry stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days,
then it is gone for good.
